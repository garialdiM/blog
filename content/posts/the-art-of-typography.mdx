Most of the web is text. Yet typography is often the last thing developers think about. A few deliberate choices can dramatically improve how readable and trustworthy your content feels.

## Measure matters

The ideal line length for body text is roughly 60–75 characters. Too wide and the eye loses its place jumping back to the next line; too narrow and reading becomes choppy.

```css
.prose {
  max-width: 65ch;
  line-height: 1.65;
}
```

## Hierarchy through contrast

You don't need many font sizes. A clear hierarchy comes from contrast in **weight**, **size**, and **spacing**:

- Large, bold headings for structure
- Comfortable body text with generous line height
- Muted color for secondary metadata like dates

## Less is more

Limit yourself to two typefaces at most. One for headings, one for body. Consistency reads as intentionality, and intentionality builds trust.

That's really the whole secret: respect the reader's eye, and get out of the way.
